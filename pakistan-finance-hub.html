Pakistan salary tax calculator: Quickly estimate monthly withholding and annual income tax for a salaried person using FY 2026–27 progressive slabs. You can edit every threshold and rate to test a future budget or a custom scenario.

Useful context, before you decide.

These calculators are planning aids. Financial products, tax filings and market transactions may include details a simple estimate cannot capture.

Which Pakistan tax year does the salary calculator use?

The default schedule is for salaried individuals in FY 2026–27: a Rs 600,000 annual tax-free threshold followed by progressive rates from 1% to 35%. You can open “Edit tax slabs” and change the annual limits and rates.

Does the EMI result include bank fees or insurance?

No. It uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, late charges, balloon payments and a bank’s own rounding can change the actual instalment.

How many grams are in one tola of gold?

This converter uses 11.6638038 grams per tola and 12 masha per tola. Jewellers may quote purity, making charges and market spreads separately from weight.

Why do I enter my own currency rate?

Exchange rates move and vary by provider. Entering the rate offered by your bank, exchange company, remittance service or card makes the result relevant without pretending that an old static rate is live.

What data leaves my browser?

Your calculator inputs stay in the current browser tab and are not saved after you close or reload it. Google Analytics receives site-usage data, such as page visits and interactions, but not the salary, loan, gold or currency values you enter.

Privacy Policy

How Pakistan Finance Hub handles calculator entries, analytics information and cookies.

Last updated: 10 October 2026. Pakistan Finance Hub is designed so you can use its financial calculators without creating an account or sending us the figures you enter.

Calculator information stays in your browser

The values you enter into the income tax, loan EMI, gold and currency calculators are processed entirely by JavaScript in your current browser tab. Pakistan Finance Hub does not upload, save or receive those calculator inputs.

  • Salary, loan, interest-rate, gold-weight and exchange-rate entries remain on your device while the page is open.
  • Your entries are not added to a user account or database.
  • Reloading or closing the page clears the values you entered during that visit.
Your calculator figures stay on your device. Google Analytics receives site-usage information, not the financial values typed into the calculators.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, with measurement ID G-71TS2905B2, to understand how the site is used and improve its tools. Google Analytics may send information to Google about visits and interactions, such as:

  • pages viewed and features used;
  • general device, browser and operating-system information;
  • referral information and approximate location derived from network information; and
  • visit timing, duration and similar site-performance or usage events.

This analytics information is handled by Google under Google’s own privacy terms. We do not intentionally include the numbers entered into calculator fields in analytics events.

Cookies and similar technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies to distinguish visits, measure usage and produce aggregated reports. Your browser controls whether cookies are accepted. You may block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although doing so can limit analytics measurement.

Pakistan Finance Hub does not use cookies to save your calculator inputs, and the calculators continue to work without an account.

External links

The site includes links to third-party sources and contact pages. Once you follow an external link, that destination’s privacy practices apply. Review its policies before sharing information there.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site, analytics setup or applicable requirements change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.
