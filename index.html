Financial Calculator FAQs — Answers for Smarter Planning

These calculators are planning aids. Financial products, tax filings and market transactions may include details a simple estimate cannot capture.

What is a SIP calculator?

A SIP calculator projects the potential future value of regular monthly investments. Enter a monthly contribution, expected annual return and investment period to compare your total contributions with estimated growth.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. SIP results are illustrations based on a constant expected return. Market volatility, fees, taxes, inflation and the timing of contributions can change the actual outcome.

How does compound interest work?

Compound interest adds earned returns to the balance. Later returns are then calculated on both the starting principal and prior growth, which can accelerate growth over longer periods.

Does compounding frequency affect the result?

At the same nominal annual rate, more frequent compounding usually produces a slightly higher result because returns are credited sooner. Compare yearly, half-yearly, quarterly and monthly options in the calculator.

What should I compare between two loans?

Compare monthly EMI, total interest, total repayment, fees, insurance and settlement terms. FinPulse highlights the lower estimated total repayment, but lender terms can change which offer is better.

Does a lower EMI always mean a cheaper loan?

No. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly instalment while increasing total interest. Review the full repayment amount as well as the monthly payment.

Are the gold and silver rates live?

No. The dashboard shows a clearly dated static snapshot for quick planning. Gold and silver prices can move during the day and vary by city, dealer, purity and transaction size.

How should I use the gold rate and unit converter together?

Use the dashboard rate as a dated reference, or enter a jeweller’s current per-tola quote in the converter. The tool converts between tola, masha and grams and then estimates value.

Which Pakistan tax year does the salary calculator use?

The default schedule is for salaried individuals in FY 2026–27: a Rs 600,000 annual tax-free threshold followed by progressive rates from 1% to 35%. You can open “Edit tax slabs” and change the annual limits and rates.

Does the EMI result include bank fees or insurance?

No. It uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, late charges, balloon payments and a bank’s own rounding can change the actual instalment.

How many grams are in one tola of gold?

This converter uses 11.6638038 grams per tola and 12 masha per tola. Jewellers may quote purity, making charges and market spreads separately from weight.

Why do I enter my own currency rate?

Exchange rates move and vary by provider. Entering the rate offered by your bank, exchange company, remittance service or card makes the result relevant without pretending that an old static rate is live.

What data leaves my browser?

Your calculator inputs stay in the current browser tab and are not saved after you close or reload it. Google Analytics receives site-usage data, such as page visits and interactions, but not the salary, loan, gold or currency values you enter.
